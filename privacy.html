Visiting this site (server logs)

This is a static site with no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device when you view it. It loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or images on page view.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). Like any web host, GitHub automatically processes the technical data your browser sends to deliver a page — including your IP address, the requested URL, and the date and time — in short-lived server logs, to serve the page and protect against abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating the site securely, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Mailing list

If you sign up for the mailing list, the email address you provide (and a language tag, to know which language to write to you in) is processed to send you occasional notice of new releases.

  • Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
  • Double opt-in: after you submit the form you receive a confirmation email and are only added to the list once you click the link in it. The confirmation (time and IP address) is recorded as proof of consent, Art. 7(1) GDPR.
  • Processor: the list is operated on our behalf by MailerLite (UAB “MailerLite”, Paupio g. 46, Vilnius, Lithuania), under a data-processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.
  • Retention: your address is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, after which it is removed (a minimal suppression record may be retained to honour your unsubscribe).
  • Withdrawing consent: you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email, or by emailing contact@annaeus.de. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data (Art. 15);
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17);
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18);
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • withdraw any consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise any of these, email contact@annaeus.de. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place, and your data is never sold.

Right to complain

You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority competent for the controller is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland (Die Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Fritz-Dobisch-Straße 12, 66111 Saarbrücken, Germany), or the authority where you live or work.
