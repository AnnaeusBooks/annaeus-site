Privacy Policy

What this site collects

This site itself collects no personal data beyond what your browser sends to load a static page (standard server logs kept by the hosting provider, GitHub Pages).

Mailing list

If you sign up for the mailing list, the email address you provide is collected and stored by MailerLite (the email service provider), who processes it on our behalf to send you notice of new releases. Sign-up uses double opt-in: you'll receive a confirmation email and must click it before you're added to the list.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email, or by contacting contact@annaeus.de to request deletion of your data.

MailerLite's own privacy policy and data processing terms apply to how they handle the data: see their site for details.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to access/delete your data: contact@annaeus.de.
